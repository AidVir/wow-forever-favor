# WoW Forever market tools

A static website with three calculators for **WoW Forever** (Blizzard's 2026 Classic-style server),
priced from the **EU PvP** auction houses:

| Page | Files | What it answers |
|---|---|---|
| Merchant's Favor | `favor.html`, `app.js` | Cheapest Waylaid Crate to fill for Merchant's Favor (gold per Favor), plus a goal planner |
| Profession leveling (home page) | `index.html`, `leveling.js` | Cheapest 1–300 route per profession, with vendor vs AH resale of what you craft |
| Craftsman's Writs | `writs.html`, `writs.js` | All 150 writs ranked by gold per 100 trade-faction reputation |

- Live site: https://aidvir.github.io/wow-forever-favor/ (GitHub Pages, served from `main`)
- Repo: https://github.com/AidVir/wow-forever-favor

No framework, no bundler, no npm dependencies. The pages are plain HTML and ES modules, and the
scripts are plain Node (`package.json` only sets `"type": "module"`).

## Data pipeline

**Blizzard Game Data API.** Host `eu.api.blizzard.com`, namespaces `dynamic-forever-eu` and
`static-forever-eu`. OAuth uses client credentials at `oauth.battle.net/token`.
- Forever has no realms, only rulesets. Ruleset ids: 1 PvP, 2 Normal, 3 RP, 15 RP-PvP.
- AH index: `/data/wow/ruleset/{id}/auctions/` (the trailing slash is required).
- Per house: `/data/wow/ruleset/{id}/auctions/{houseId}` and `.../{houseId}/commodities`.
- EU PvP houses: Alliance 110, Horde 113, Blackwater (neutral) 116. These were verified against
  another site's prices and are pinned in `data/houses.json`.
- Materials are commodities priced with `unit_price`. Other items use `buyout` for the whole stack.

**`fetch-prices.mjs`** works in four steps:
1. **Skip check.** A conditional request (If-Modified-Since) stops the job when Blizzard has no new
   snapshot. Snapshots come about once an hour.
2. **Scan.** Otherwise it scans the three houses for every wanted item (crate items, every item in
   `recipes.json`, writ items).
3. **Write prices.** `prices.json` keeps up to 1,500 of the cheapest units per item, plus a 72-entry
   price history for crate items.
4. **Track sales.** The API never reports sales, so the job compares this snapshot's listings with
   the previous snapshot's:
   - The previous snapshot's listings live in `.cache/ah-state.json` (Actions cache, gitignored).
   - A listing that had LONG/VERY_LONG time left (2+ hours) and is gone, or smaller, less than
     2 hours later counts as sold. A seller cancelling looks the same.
   - Gaps longer than 2 hours are not counted.
   - Results go to `data/sales.json`, kept for 7 days: `factions.{f}.items[id] = [[hour, units, copper]]`
     and `hours` = snapshot time covered.
5. **Track market trends.** Per item a robust market price (average of the cheapest 10% of listed
   units, 5–200 units, so one cheap listing can't move it) and the units listed. History is one point
   per 3 h for 7 days in `.cache/market.json` (Actions cache); `data/trends.json` publishes
   `factions.{f}[id] = [price now, 24 h ago, 72 h ago, units now, 24 h ago, 72 h ago]` (null = no
   history yet) and `hours` of history per faction.

**Client trend model** (`common.js`): `marketTrend()` gives `{pct, perDay, supply, src}` from
`trends.json` (72 h → daily rate, else 24 h), falling back to sold prices in `sales.json` (counted at
half). `projectPrice(price, trend, days)` only projects falls (max −50%/day, floor 40% of today).

**Workflow (`update-prices.yml`).**
- **Why it loops:** GitHub's cron was unreliable on this repo, so a manual or scheduled run loops
  every 10 minutes for about 5.5 hours, committing whenever data changes. It then starts the next
  run itself with `gh workflow run`.
- **Backup cron** (`17 */6 * * *`) restarts the chain if it ever breaks.
- **Push runs** (changes to scripts or data definitions) scan once with `FORCE=1`.
- **Starting the chain:** `gh workflow run update-prices.yml -f minutes=0` makes a short run that
  hands off to a full one.
- **Concurrency:** `group: update-prices` means only one run at a time.
- **Bot commits:** commits made by the bot are named `Update AH prices …`. Expect `git pull --rebase`
  before every push.

**`build-recipes.mjs`** joins:
- the Blizzard API (recipes and reagents; the Blizzard recipe id equals the SkillLineAbility id),
- wago.tools DB2 CSVs for client build 1.60.1.70862 (SkillLineAbility colours, ItemSparse patterns,
  SpellCastingRequirements/SpellFocusObject stations, SpellCooldowns),
- nexus-devs `wow-classic-items` (trainer skill, `vendorPrice`, `createdBy`).

It drops:
- Season of Discovery recipes,
- recipes with cooldowns of 10 minutes or more,
- new Forever recipes with no known source.

It keeps no-skill intermediates such as cloth bolts. It includes Mining (186) as a helper profession
for smelting. Enchant trainer skills with no data are estimated and flagged `est`.

## Game rules the code relies on

- **Waylaid Crates:** raw crates give 5/10/15 Favor and crafted crates 10/20/30; the Artisan values
  20/40 are estimates. Performance Bonus gives a 5/10/15% chance of double Favor. Item ids are
  248682–248713.
- **Craftsman's Writs:**
  - Reputation by rank: Journeyman 75, Expert 125, Artisan 200.
  - Writs are tradeable, give no Favor, and each one can be completed once per day.
  - Item ids are 264011–264160.
  - Sealed envelopes are 262888/262889/262890.
- **Skill-up chance:** orange (below yellow) always gives a point. Otherwise the chance is
  (grey − skill) / (grey − yellow); green = average of yellow and grey.

## Leveling planner (leveling.js)

- **Per-point greedy route.** For each skill point it picks the recipe with the lowest expected cost.
- **Reagent cost** is the cheapest of three options:
  - vendor,
  - AH (listings are consumed from the order book as they are bought),
  - crafting the intermediate yourself, up to 2 levels deep.
- **Pattern cost** is spread over about 5 skill points. A time cost per craft is added (default 5s).
- **Route shape rules** (constants near the top of `leveling.js`):
  - `STICK` / `STICK_MIN`: stay on the current recipe unless the best one is >10% (≥50c/point) cheaper.
  - `MAX_CRAFTS_PER_POINT` (3): with "Avoid recipes needing over 3 crafts per point" on, slower
    recipes are skipped while a faster one exists.
- **Training ranks:** `RANKS` (75/150/225 caps, skill 50/125/200, level 10/20/35). Steps break at each
  cap, a "Train X" row is shown with `TRAINERS` (Forever Expert/Artisan trainers per faction), and
  "Character level" ends the route at the last rank that level can train. The shopping list says how
  much is needed before 225 (Artisan needs level 35).
- **Tools:** `recipe.tools` = required tool categories (from the client's `SpellTotems`); top-level
  `recipes.json` `tools.categories[c] = {n, ok:[itemIds that satisfy it]}`, `tools.items[id] = {cat, bop, vendor}`.
  The route gets each tool once, the first time a step needs it (`toolsNeeded` / `toolGet`): vendor
  (`VENDOR_TOOLS` in `scripts/client-extras.mjs`), AH (not for BoP rods) or crafted with this profession.
  Its cost is amortised like a pattern; the step shows "first: buy/craft X".
- **Inventory:** step outputs go into lots (`lots`, `takeInv`); later steps use them before buying
  (`acquire` takes `ctx.inv`, valued at the sale credit you give up). Using an item undoes its sale
  credit, so totals stay cash-correct. Steps show "keep N for later" / "uses N from earlier steps".
- **Two passes:** `render()` calls `plan(plan().demand)`. The second pass credits crafting a reagent
  the first pass needed at 90% of what buying it costs (`replaceUnit`, up to the remaining demand), so
  intermediates get made early for skill points, as guides do with bolts / grinding stones / powder.
- **Alternatives:** every usable recipe at a skill point is a candidate (`cands`); the step keeps the
  runner-up at its first point (a fast one when "avoid slow recipes" is on) and adds up
  `(alt.run − best.run)` over the points where it still works (`run` = per-point cost without the
  one-time pattern/tool cost, shown separately). `altFact()` renders it; "Use it instead" excludes
  the step's recipe (same as ✕).
- **Pattern sources:** `recipe.src` = `{k:"vendor", npc, cost, faction}` | `{k:"quest", quest}` |
  `{k:"drop", from}` | `{k:"new"}` (Forever-only, not in Classic data), from wow-classic-items.
  "Recipes I can use" (listed first = default): `buyable` (AH / vendor / quest), `vendor`, `trainer`,
  `all` (every pattern owned, unknown ones free; an old saved `all` is moved to `buyable` once via
  `lv:patternsV2` / `mm:patternsV2`); `vendor` = vendor + quest patterns, no drops. `scripts/client-extras.mjs` writes `tools`
  and `src` without Blizzard credentials (`node scripts/client-extras.mjs`); `build-recipes.mjs` runs it too.
- **Learn skill:** trainer recipes use Classic's learn skill shifted by how far Forever moved the
  yellow threshold (`scripts/learn-levels.mjs`, also used by `build-recipes.mjs`). Run
  `node scripts/learn-levels.mjs` to re-apply it to `recipes.json` without Blizzard credentials.
- **Resale of crafted items:** `saleModel()`.
  - **With 6+ hours of sales history:** sell up to `perDay × waitDays × 50%` on the AH. The price is
    the lower of the cheapest listing and the average sold price, projected to mid selling time
    (`wait/2 + 0.5` days) when falling, times 0.95 (the AH cut). Above `CROWD_DAYS` (2) days of supply
    listed, your 50% share shrinks in proportion. The sell list shows trends and crowded markets; the
    page checks for new prices every 10 minutes and offers "Re-plan with them" (it never re-plans by itself).
  - **Before that:** fall back to up to 25% of the currently listed units, at the cheapest listing.
  - **The rest** is vendored. Vendor credit is capped at 75% of cost in route scoring.
- **Scoring of profitable crafts:** a craft that makes money is scored by its profit per craft, not
  divided by its skill-up chance. Otherwise low-chance crafts win.
- **Sign convention in the UI:** profit is `+` (green, `.pos`) and loss is `−` (red, `.neg`), via
  `pl()`. Plain costs stay unsigned.

## Working on it

- **Preview locally:**
  - `node scripts/serve.mjs` (or double-click `serve.cmd`), then open http://localhost:5178/.
  - Responses are sent with `no-store`, so a reload always shows your edits.
  - `prices.json` and `sales.json` are proxied live from GitHub raw; `--local-data` uses the local files.
  - The live site is cached by GitHub Pages for about 10 minutes after a push.
- **Tools on this PC:** the user has no admin rights. Node and gh are portable installs under
  `%LOCALAPPDATA%\portable-tools\` (`node-v24.11.0-win-x64\node.exe`, `gh\bin\gh.exe`, logged in as
  AidVir). `node` is not on PATH; git is.
- **Secrets:** Battle.net credentials exist only as the repo secrets `BLIZZARD_CLIENT_ID` and
  `BLIZZARD_CLIENT_SECRET`. Never write them into files, commits or logs. To fetch prices locally,
  set them as environment variables for the command.
- **Checks before pushing:**
  - `node --check` any script you touched.
  - Load all three pages and check that the browser console shows no errors.
  - Check both phone width and dark/light mode.
- **Styling rules:**
  - Keep everything in `style.css` and reuse the tokens (`--surface`, `--accent`, `--good`, `--bad`, …),
    plus the spacing / type scale (`--sp-1`…`--sp-7`, `--fs-xs`…`--fs-3xl`) and `--header-h`.
  - Fonts are Inter (UI) and Cinzel (headings only; numbers stay in Inter).
  - Class names are shared across pages and generated in JS, so search the JS before renaming a class.
    Add new names instead of renaming old ones.
- **Page structure** (same on every page, copy it for a new page):
  - `<a class="skip">`, then `<header class="top"><div class="wrap bar">`: brand (`.brand`, links home),
    `<nav class="tabs">` with one `<a>` per tool (`aria-current="page"` on the current one, short labels),
    faction `.seg`. The header is sticky on desktop and static on phones.
  - `<main id="main">` starts with `<section class="intro">`: `.eyebrow`, `<h1>`, `.lead`, then
    `.intro-meta` holding `#status` (short: "Prices updated …") and `.pill#market`.
  - Answer first: `.hero` (big number + `.hero-alt` list of `.alt` rows), then the settings panel.
  - Settings: `.panel.settings` with the 2–4 controls people change most in `.controls`; everything
    else goes in `<details class="more" open>` ("More options", open by default, + a `<small>` listing what's inside).
  - Long explanations go in `<details class="explain">` (collapsed), not in fine print under tables.
  - `<footer class="site-foot"><div class="wrap foot">`: about, tools links, project links.
- **Tables:**
  - Wrap in `.table-scroll`; add `.sticky` for long tables (sticky `<th>` under the header, desktop only).
  - Keep secondary numbers out of the row: put them in an expandable `tr.detail` (rows are
    `tr.row` with `tabindex="0"`, `aria-expanded`, toggled by click / Enter / Space).
  - `table.cards` turns rows into cards below 760 px. Each table defines its own grid areas in the
    phone media query (`table.rank`, `table.writs`, `table.steps`), cells use `c-*` classes and
    `data-label` for the small label shown on phones. Other tables just scroll inside `.table-scroll`.
  - Tiers are a coloured dot + word (`.tier.t-…`), not filled badges.

## Known limits

- Launch-week markets are thin. Some leveling routes stop before 300 when the AH runs out of a reagent.
- Trainer fees are not included.
- Some vendor prices come from Classic data.
- AH resale is an estimate:
  - Cancelled listings are counted as sales.
  - Deposits and undercutting aren't modelled.
- Artisan crate Favor values are estimates.

## Money making (money.html / money.js / money.css)

Ranks every craft the user can make (ticked professions at their skill; none ticked = all at 300) by
sustainable profit per day. The constants at the top of `money.js` are the rules, and the page repeats
them under "How this is calculated", so change both together. `money.css` holds page-only rules on top
of the `style.css` tokens. `acquire()` and `salesStats()` at the end of `common.js` are its cost and
demand models (`leveling.js` still has its own copies and could switch to them).
- **Sell price:** min(cheapest listing, average sold) on the faction AH, projected `SELL_DAYS` (1) ahead
  when falling (flag `price falling` at −5%/day; `supply rising` at +50% listed in 24 h), × 0.95, minus a lost deposit of
  15% of vendor sell price (×2 when oversupplied); the vendor price if higher. Blackwater only for buying.
- **Reagent cost:** walks the order book for a whole day's crafts; crafts intermediates when cheaper;
  "I gather my own" values reagents at AH sell value (or 0). Crafts/day are capped by AH reagent supply.
- **Demand:** sales/day × share; share shrinks above 2 days of supply, flagged oversupplied above 3.
  Before 6 h of history: a quarter of listed units per day, marked `guess`.
- **Flags:** `risk` (few sales, one listing, price spike >1.5×) hides the row unless "Show unreliable
  markets"; `warn` / `info` only label it. Enchantments, never-traded items (no binding data, maybe BoP)
  and cooldown crafts are not ranked. Data reloads every 10 minutes while the tab is open.
