About the data

Recipes, reagents and skill-up colours come from the live WoW Forever client (via the Blizzard Game Data API and wago.tools client tables), so Forever's own changes are included — for example sharpening stones turn grey much earlier than in Classic. Trainer skill requirements and vendor prices come from Classic data where Forever didn't change them. Forever turned many recipes into patterns (many bought with Merchant's Favor from the faction camp vendors); pattern steps are tagged, with the auction house price when one is listed. Switch "Recipes I can use" to limit the route to trainer recipes or patterns you can buy. Some recipes need crafting stations such as an Anvil, Forge, Tanning Rack or Sewing Machine.
