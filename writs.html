How Craftsman's Writs work

  1. Get a writ. Sealed Journeyman's, Expert's and Artisan's Writ envelopes drop from mobs from level 10. Opening one gives a random writ of that rank. Writs and envelopes are tradeable, so you can also buy them on the auction house.
  2. Deliver the item. Each writ starts a quest to deliver one crafted item (Crafted Solid Shot needs 200) to an NPC. You don't need the profession: buying the item works too.
  3. Reward. Journeyman 75, Expert 125, Artisan 200 reputation with the Azeroth Commerce Authority (Alliance) or Durotar Supply and Logistics (Horde), often plus a small coin pouch (about 35s for Journeyman). Writs give no Merchant's Favor.
  4. Limits. Each specific writ can be completed once per day; different writs can be done the same day. A Forger's Quill (15 Merchant's Favor at Honored) rerolls a writ into another of the same rank.
  5. Your first crate or writ also completes Shipping Label: 2,000 reputation and 50 Merchant's Favor, once per character.
