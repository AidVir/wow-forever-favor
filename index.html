How Waylaid Crates work
  1. Get a crate. Waylaid Crates drop from mobs anywhere from level 10, or you can buy them on the auction house (empty crates are not bound).
  2. Fill it. Right-click to read the label, then right-click again and pick one of the accepted bundles from your bags. It becomes a soulbound Sealed Crate.
  3. Turn it in. Alliance: Marcy Baker, Azeroth Commerce Authority camp at Three Corners, Redridge. Horde: Dokimi, Durotar Supply and Logistics camp west of the Crossroads, The Barrens.
  4. Favor paid. Gathering crates (herbs, ore, textiles, curiosities) pay 5 / 10 / 15 for Apprentice / Journeyman / Expert. Crafted crates (fabrics, ingots, parts) pay double: 10 / 20 / 30. Your first crate also completes Shipping Label (+50 Favor, 2,000 rep, once per character).
  5. Performance Bonus (Legacy, professions) gives a 5 / 10 / 15% chance to double the Favor from a turn-in; the table uses the expected value.

About the prices

Prices are what it costs to buy the exact quantity from the cheapest listings right now, from the official Blizzard Game Data API. The site checks every 10 minutes; Blizzard publishes a new snapshot about once an hour. Artisan (level 35) Favor values are not published yet; they are estimated at 20 (gathering) and 40 (crafted) and marked with est. Crate and bundle data comes from community guides of the beta and launch week (Zockify, Forever Mastery, WoW Forever Builds).
